# Source

This plugin is **third-party**, not authored by us. Vendored here for convenience/reproducibility only.

- Upstream: https://github.com/shezik/SuspendHack
- Purpose on this device: lets the Kindle suspend normally even with the Amazon framework disabled (see `kindle/koreader-plugins/networkextras.koplugin`'s "Framework Mode" feature, which runs the device frameworkless most of the time). Without this, suspend/resume behaves differently or fails once the framework is off.
